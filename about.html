Our story

Clothing that feels as good as it looks

Threadly started with one simple idea: great everyday clothes shouldn't cost the earth — or the planet.

Since
2019

Who we are

Designed in India, loved everywhere

We're a team of designers, pattern-makers and fabric nerds who believe clothing should be comfortable first and clever second. Every Threadly piece starts as a sketch, becomes a sample, then gets worn, washed and tested by real people before it ever reaches you.

The result is a wardrobe of easy, colourful pieces that mix and match — from the first chilly morning to the last long evening.

What we stand for

Our values

  • Made to last

    We obsess over stitching, seams and fabric weights so your favourites survive wash after wash.

  • Kinder to the planet

    Organic cotton, recycled fibres and low-impact dyes — a little better with every collection.

  • Fair, always

    Honest prices, no inflated markups and partner factories that pay and treat people properly.

Our journey

From five tees to a full wardrobe

  1. 2019

    A small studio

    Threadly begins in a tiny Ahmedabad studio with five tees and a big idea.

  2. 2021

    First flagship store

    We open our first store and start designing for kids and women, too.

  3. 2024

    Better fabrics

    Every cotton style moves to certified organic or recycled fibres.

  4. 2026

    Today

    250+ styles, thousands of happy customers and the same obsession with fit and feel.
