Shipping

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days across India. Express delivery (1–2 working days) is available in major cities at checkout.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is free on orders over ₹1,999. Below that, a flat fee of ₹99 applies.

Can I track my order?

Yes. As soon as your order ships we email and text you a tracking link.

Returns & exchanges

What is your return policy?

You can return any unworn, unwashed item with tags attached within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

How do I start a return?

Contact us with your order number and we'll arrange a free pick-up. Refunds reach your original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Can I exchange for a different size?

Absolutely — exchanges are free and we'll send the new size as soon as your return is picked up.

Sizing & fit

How do I find my size?

Use the size chart below. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit or down for a closer fit.

Do your clothes shrink?

Our cotton is pre-washed, so shrinkage is minimal. Follow the care label for best results.

Payments

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, all major credit and debit cards, popular wallets, net banking and cash on delivery.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes — payments are processed through encrypted, PCI-compliant gateways. We never store your card details.
